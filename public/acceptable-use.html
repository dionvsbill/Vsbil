Acceptable Use Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

This policy defines prohibited uses of VSBIL systems, accounts, APIs, campaigns, payments and reward mechanisms. It supplements the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

1. Lawful use

You may use VSBIL only for lawful purposes and only in ways permitted by these policies and the rules of connected third-party platforms.

2. Account abuse

  • No fake or deceptive identities.
  • No account farms created to multiply rewards.
  • No credential sharing or account takeover.
  • No circumvention of suspension or eligibility controls.
  • No sale or transfer of access for abusive purposes.

3. Technical abuse

You must not probe, scan, overload, exploit, reverse engineer or interfere with VSBIL systems except where expressly permitted by a documented security program. This includes attempts to bypass authentication, authorization, rate limits, payment verification, wallet controls, campaign allocation or fraud detection.

4. Automation

Automation intended to inflate activity, submit fraudulent proof, create accounts, manipulate rewards or evade platform controls is prohibited. Legitimate accessibility tools are not prohibited merely because they automate an input; however, they may not be used to defeat marketplace integrity or third-party platform rules.

5. Financial abuse

Do not use stolen payment instruments, false payment claims, chargeback schemes, withdrawal duplication, ledger manipulation, referral fraud or other techniques intended to obtain money or coins improperly.

6. Campaign abuse

Campaigns must not contain malware, phishing, credential collection, deceptive redirects, illegal offers, false financial promises or instructions intended to violate another platform's policies.

7. Data abuse

Do not harvest another user's private information, expose private URLs or attempt to obtain passwords, tokens, verification codes or payment credentials.

8. Enforcement

VSBIL may investigate suspected violations, preserve relevant security records, restrict features, reject activity, reverse improper rewards, suspend accounts, cancel campaigns or terminate access. Where appropriate and lawful, serious abuse may be reported to relevant providers or authorities.

9. Responsible security research

Good-faith vulnerability reporting should use the designated security contact and avoid accessing, modifying or deleting other users' data. See Security & Responsible Disclosure.

10. Updates

This policy may be updated as VSBIL adds APIs, advertising, payment methods and new marketplace features.
