WALLET & PAYOUTS

Withdrawal Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

1. Current limits

Minimum withdrawal: ₵20. Maximum: ₵5,000 within a rolling seven-day period. These limits are platform configuration values and may be changed prospectively.

2. Eligibility

A withdrawal requires an active account, sufficient available balance and compliance with applicable verification and risk controls. Suspended accounts or accounts under a legitimate financial/security investigation may be temporarily restricted.

3. Submission

When you submit a withdrawal, VSBIL records the amount, currency equivalent, selected payout provider and relevant request information. You are responsible for entering accurate payout details.

4. Funds reservation

The requested amount may move from available funds into a pending/reserved state while the request is reviewed. This prevents the same coins from being requested twice while preserving an auditable balance history.

5. Verification

Normal verification is targeted at approximately 2–3 business days. Verification can take longer where additional evidence is required, suspicious activity is detected, payment-provider checks are pending or a provider experiences delays.

6. Financial integrity checks

Before approval, VSBIL may check account status, weekly withdrawal totals, available balance, ledger consistency, reward history, referral history, previous reversals and security/risk signals. A discrepancy may cause the request to enter manual review.

7. Approval and payout

Approval means the request passed VSBIL's review; payout may still depend on the selected provider. A request should be marked paid only after the provider result is appropriately recorded.

8. Rejection

A withdrawal may be rejected because of insufficient balance, invalid payout information, weekly limits, account restrictions, unresolved verification, suspected fraud or another applicable policy reason. Reserved funds from a rejected request should be returned to the available balance through an auditable ledger movement.

9. Failed payouts

If a provider fails a payout, VSBIL may place the request into a failed state and return the affected amount to available funds after reconciliation. Users may be asked to correct payout details.

10. No instant-payout guarantee

The published 2–3 business-day period is a normal verification target, not a promise that every payment will arrive within exactly that period.

11. Prohibited manipulation

Users must not split withdrawals to evade limits, create accounts to bypass controls, manipulate ledger data, exploit race conditions or submit duplicate payout requests.

12. Support

For withdrawal issues, contact support with the withdrawal reference. Never send your payment PIN, password or one-time authentication code.
