VSBIL POLICY

Withdrawal Policy

Withdrawals are subject to the minimum and maximum limits shown in your account, available balance, account status, verification requirements and administrative review.

Review and payout

Submitting a withdrawal reserves the requested amount. Rejected withdrawals are returned to the available balance. Approved requests are paid through the selected method and marked paid after payout.

Accuracy

You are responsible for providing accurate mobile-money or bank details. Vsbil may delay or reject a request where details are incomplete or suspicious.
