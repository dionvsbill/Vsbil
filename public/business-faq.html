VSBIL BUSINESS HELP

Business Suite FAQ

Answers for shops, e-commerce orders, WhatsApp automation, invoices, landlords, churches and funeral contribution pages.

How much is the shop?

The Shop Inventory plan is GH₵150/month. Publishing a storefront requires an active shop subscription.

How much is WhatsApp automation?

WhatsApp Starter is GH₵150/month. The Shop + WhatsApp bundle is GH₵250/month, saving GH₵50 compared with the two individual monthly prices.

Can customers buy from my shop?

Yes. A published shop has a public storefront with product prices, discounts, stock availability and checkout.

Can customers track their orders?

Yes. Each successful order receives an order number. Customers can use the order number and buyer phone number at the order-tracking page to see status, items, payment state and the delivery timeline.

Are discounts calculated securely?

Yes. The storefront may display discounts, but checkout prices are recalculated on the server. Stock is reserved through an atomic database function to reduce overselling during concurrent orders.

Can my shop connect to WhatsApp?

Yes. After connecting a WhatsApp Business bot and activating both subscriptions, a shop owner can link the bot from Shop Admin. The bot can be used for customer automation and order-related workflows.

Do I give VSBIL my WhatsApp password?

No. The connection uses Meta-issued Phone Number ID and access-token credentials. VSBIL stores the token encrypted server-side and does not intentionally return it to the browser.

Who is responsible for customer messages?

The business owner is responsible for lawful customer communications, consent, product claims and compliance with Meta/WhatsApp policies. VSBIL provides automation infrastructure.

Can I sell anything?

No. Counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, illegal, deceptive or prohibited goods and services are not allowed.

What does the invoice tool do?

It creates structured GHS invoices with line items, tax/discount fields, client details, due dates and payment status records.

What does the landlord tool do?

It records properties, tenants, rent cycles, utility amounts and payment records. It is a management tool, not legal advice.

What does the church tracker do?

It records offering entries, methods and totals for reporting. It is a bookkeeping aid and does not become custodian of church funds.

How do funeral contributions work?

A family organizer creates a public campaign. Supporters can contribute through the payment provider. When enabled, VSBIL may retain a disclosed 3% platform fee from successfully verified contributions.

Are payments trusted from the browser?

No. VSBIL verifies supported payments server-side with the payment provider before activating subscriptions or marking supported financial states as paid.
