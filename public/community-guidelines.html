LEGAL & TRUST CENTER

Community Guidelines

Version 1.2 · Effective August 31, 2026

These rules apply across VSBIL's social feed, creator program, campaigns, YouTube activities, Marketplace, shops, messaging, WhatsApp automation and business tools.

1. Be authentic

Use genuine accounts and genuine participation. Bots, click farms, automated browsers, account farms, purchased engagement and coordinated manipulation of campaigns or creator metrics are prohibited.

2. Content quality and originality

Creators must submit content they have the right to use. VSBIL may evaluate originality, meaningful human contribution, quality, duplication, engagement integrity and safety before content receives broad distribution or creator earnings. AI-assisted material is not automatically prohibited, but repetitive, unedited, copied or low-value material may be excluded from monetization.

3. Copyright and intellectual property

Do not upload material you do not have permission to use. VSBIL may restrict content after credible copyright complaints, rights-holder notices or strong infringement signals. Repeat infringement can result in loss of publishing or earning privileges and account enforcement.

4. Sexual and harmful content

Nudity, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, sexualized abuse, grooming and other prohibited sexual material are not allowed. Safety systems may automatically block or route suspected content for review. Serious threats, exploitation and dangerous wrongdoing may be removed and reported where appropriate.

5. Shops and marketplace trust

Shop owners must accurately represent products, prices, stock, delivery, returns and business identity. Counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, deceptive or unlawful goods are prohibited. Users should report suspicious shops or products. VSBIL verification badges represent completion of the applicable VSBIL verification process, not an unconditional guarantee of every seller or item.

6. Reviews and testimonials

Reviews must reflect genuine experiences. Do not buy, fabricate, coordinate or manipulate reviews, testimonials, ratings or recommendations. Businesses must not retaliate against customers for honest reviews.

7. Messages and WhatsApp automation

Messages must be relevant, lawful and respectful. WhatsApp automation must be used only with appropriate customer expectations and permissions. Spam, phishing, credential collection, scams, harassment and attempts to evade provider enforcement are prohibited.

8. Campaigns and referrals

Campaign creators must provide accurate requirements and rewards. Participants must complete tasks honestly. Never request passwords, OTPs, payment PINs or private authentication credentials. Referral rewards apply only when the active qualification rules are met; creating fake accounts to trigger rewards is prohibited.

9. Fraud and platform abuse

  • Fake payment confirmations or altered transaction evidence.
  • Client-side, console, API or script attempts to manufacture wallet balances or rewards.
  • Withdrawal abuse, chargeback abuse or duplicate payout requests.
  • Ban evasion, rate-limit bypassing or unauthorized access attempts.
  • Impersonation, phishing, malware or credential theft.
  • Artificial engagement or manipulation of advertising or creator metrics.

10. Privacy and personal information

Do not publish another person's private information without a lawful reason and appropriate permission. Do not dox, threaten, stalk or expose private contact information. Respect account privacy and shop customer information.

11. Enforcement

VSBIL may remove content, reduce distribution, pause monetization, reverse ineligible rewards, suspend campaigns, unpublish shops, disconnect bots, restrict messaging, hold withdrawals for review or terminate accounts depending on severity and evidence. Automated signals can trigger review; they are not a declaration of criminal guilt.

12. Reporting and appeals

Use Support / Reports for fake shops, unsafe content, copyright concerns, harassment, scams, account compromise or suspicious campaigns. Where an appeal is offered, provide accurate evidence and context. Abuse or fabricated evidence may result in additional restrictions.

13. Updates

These guidelines may be updated as VSBIL features, safety requirements, laws and third-party platform policies evolve.
