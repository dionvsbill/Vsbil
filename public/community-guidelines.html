Community Guidelines

Version 1.1 · Effective August 30, 2026

VSBIL connects creators, participants, businesses and communities through genuine participation and practical digital tools. These guidelines apply to campaigns, YouTube activities, shops, customer communications, WhatsApp automation and business services.

1. Authentic participation

Use accounts you control and participate because you genuinely choose to. Bots, scripts, click farms, automated browsers and coordinated systems designed to manufacture activity or defeat verification are prohibited.

2. Campaign integrity

Campaign creators must describe requested activity accurately and must not ask for passwords, authentication codes, payment PINs or private credentials.

3. YouTube integrity

Users must follow YouTube rules. No campaign may instruct users to deceive YouTube or evade enforcement. VSBIL does not guarantee that an activity counts toward YouTube monetization or eligibility metrics.

4. Business and shop integrity

Shop owners must publish truthful product descriptions, prices, stock status and delivery information. Counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, illegal or prohibited goods may not be offered. Do not use VSBIL storefronts to impersonate another business or mislead customers.

5. Customer communications and WhatsApp

WhatsApp automation must be used for lawful, relevant communications to people who can reasonably expect to receive them. Do not use VSBIL to spam, bulk-message unwilling recipients, harvest credentials, impersonate organizations, send scams or evade Meta enforcement. Users connecting a WhatsApp number are responsible for consent, notices and compliance with Meta's policies.

6. Fraud and manipulation

  • Fake screenshots or altered evidence.
  • False task-completion claims.
  • Self-referrals and referral rings.
  • Payment manipulation or chargeback abuse.
  • Withdrawal manipulation or duplicate payout requests.
  • Automated or fabricated engagement.
  • Attempts to exploit API, database or client-side behavior.

7. Safety and respectful conduct

Do not harass, threaten, stalk, intimidate, exploit or deliberately expose private information. Content facilitating serious wrongdoing, fraud, exploitation or violence may be removed and reported where appropriate.

8. Prohibited campaigns and services

Campaigns involving scams, deceptive financial promises, illegal products or services, credential harvesting, malware, impersonation, exploitative material, prohibited adult content, hateful abuse or platform manipulation are not permitted.

9. Privacy and credentials

Never request another user's Google password, YouTube password, VSBIL password, payment PIN or one-time security code. Secure OAuth or provider flows should be used instead of collecting provider credentials through campaign forms.

10. Fair access

Campaigns may not be shown to every user. VSBIL may use eligibility, budgets, frequency controls, trust signals, rotation and capacity limits to distribute opportunities fairly. Users must not bypass allocation controls.

11. Reporting and enforcement

Report suspicious campaigns, fraud, harassment, account compromise, unsafe links, misleading shops or abusive messages through support. Depending on severity, VSBIL may reject content, reverse rewards, unpublish shops, disconnect bots, suspend features or terminate accounts.

12. Appeals

Where an appeal is available, provide relevant evidence. Fabricated evidence or repeated abusive appeals may result in additional restrictions.

13. Updates

These guidelines may evolve as the community, technology, legal requirements and third-party policies change.
