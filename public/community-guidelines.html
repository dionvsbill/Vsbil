LEGAL & TRUST CENTER

Community Guidelines

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

VSBIL is designed to connect creators, participants and future advertisers through transparent campaigns and genuine human participation. These guidelines explain the behavior expected from everyone using the platform.

1. Authentic participation

Use accounts that you control and participate because you genuinely choose to do so. Do not use bots, scripts, click farms, automated browsers, emulators or coordinated systems designed to manufacture activity or defeat VSBIL verification.

2. Campaign integrity

Campaign creators must describe the requested activity accurately, provide a legitimate destination and set an honest reward. Campaigns must not ask participants to provide passwords, authentication codes, payment PINs or private credentials.

3. YouTube integrity

Users must follow YouTube's rules when interacting with YouTube content. VSBIL does not guarantee that an activity will count toward YouTube monetization, watch-time eligibility, subscriber eligibility or other platform metrics. No campaign may instruct users to deceive YouTube or evade its enforcement systems.

4. Identity and accounts

Do not impersonate individuals, brands, public institutions or other users. Do not sell, rent or transfer accounts where doing so violates VSBIL rules or third-party requirements. Multiple accounts used to obtain duplicate rewards are prohibited.

5. Fraud and manipulation

  • Fake screenshots or altered evidence.
  • False statements about completed tasks.
  • Self-referrals and referral rings.
  • Payment manipulation or chargeback abuse.
  • Withdrawal manipulation or duplicate payout requests.
  • Automated or fabricated engagement.
  • Attempts to exploit API, database or client-side behavior.

6. Safety and respectful conduct

Do not harass, threaten, stalk, intimidate, exploit or deliberately expose private information about another person. Content that facilitates serious wrongdoing, fraud, exploitation or violence may be removed and reported where appropriate.

7. Prohibited campaign categories

Campaigns involving scams, deceptive financial promises, illegal products or services, credential harvesting, malware, impersonation, exploitative material, prohibited adult content, hateful abuse or attempts to manipulate third-party platforms are not permitted.

8. Privacy and credentials

Never request another user's Google password, YouTube password, VSBIL password, payment PIN or one-time security code. A legitimate VSBIL flow will use secure authentication or OAuth rather than asking you to paste provider credentials into a campaign.

9. Fair access

Campaigns may not be shown to every user. VSBIL may use eligibility, campaign budgets, frequency controls, trust signals, rotation and capacity limits to distribute opportunities fairly. Users must not attempt to bypass allocation controls.

10. Reporting

Users should report suspicious campaigns, fraud, harassment, account compromise, unsafe links or misleading instructions through the support channel. Reports should be truthful and sufficiently detailed for investigation.

11. Enforcement

Depending on severity, evidence and history, VSBIL may warn a user, reject a submission, remove a campaign, reverse an improper reward, place a withdrawal under review, restrict features, suspend an account or permanently terminate access.

12. Appeals

Where an appeal is available, users may provide relevant evidence. Appeals are reviewed according to available records and platform policy. Fabricated evidence or repeated abusive appeals may result in additional restrictions.

13. Safety over speed

Financial and trust decisions may take longer when additional verification is required. VSBIL prioritizes accurate accounting and user safety over instant processing.

14. Updates

These guidelines may evolve as the community, technology, legal requirements and third-party platform policies change. The version and effective date identify the current edition.
