YOUTUBE PARTICIPATION

YouTube Participation Guidelines

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

Purpose

VSBIL provides campaign and discovery tools that may reference YouTube content. The goal is genuine human participation, not artificial inflation of views, likes, subscribers or watch time.

1. Use real accounts

Participants must use accounts they control and are authorized to operate. Account farms, purchased accounts, credential sharing and deceptive identity practices are prohibited.

2. No automation

Do not use bots, scripts, click farms, automated browsers, emulators or other mechanisms designed to generate or manipulate YouTube engagement or VSBIL rewards.

3. Genuine choice

Participants should make their own decisions about content and should not be instructed to misrepresent their experience. Campaign instructions must not request passwords, authentication codes or other credentials.

4. Verification limitations

VSBIL may use authorized YouTube APIs and other server-side checks where available. APIs can have permission, privacy, quota and data-availability limitations. A successful VSBIL task record does not mean that YouTube has accepted the activity as valid monetization activity.

5. Monetization disclaimer

VSBIL does not guarantee YouTube Partner Program eligibility, monetization approval, valid public watch time, valid subscribers, views, likes, recommendations or any other YouTube outcome. YouTube makes its own decisions under its policies.

6. Reversals and monitoring

If available evidence indicates that a submission was fraudulent, duplicated, reversed or otherwise invalid, VSBIL may reject or reverse the associated platform reward. A YouTube connection may be marked expired, revoked or unhealthy when authorization fails.

7. Creator responsibilities

Creators must publish lawful campaigns with accurate descriptions and must not design tasks to circumvent YouTube enforcement or encourage policy violations. Creators are responsible for the content and destination they submit.

8. Disconnecting YouTube

Users can disconnect the integration where the feature is provided and can revoke authorization through Google. Some YouTube-dependent functions will stop when authorization is unavailable.

9. Enforcement

Violations can result in task rejection, reward reversal, campaign removal, withdrawal review, feature restrictions, suspension or termination.
