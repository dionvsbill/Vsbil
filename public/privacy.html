LEGAL & TRUST CENTER

Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Effective August 31, 2026

This Policy explains how VSBIL processes information when you use accounts, the social feed, creator earning features, campaigns, YouTube activities, referrals, wallet and withdrawals, Marketplace and shops, orders, WhatsApp automation, advertising, Business Suite and support.

1. Information collected

Depending on the feature, VSBIL may process account details, profile information, posts and media, follows and interactions, campaign/activity records, reward and referral records, payment references, wallet ledger entries, withdrawal details, shop/catalogue information, order information, support communications and security telemetry.

2. Security and abuse signals

To protect users and financial systems, VSBIL may record IP address, user-agent/device or browser signals, timestamps, request identifiers, session information, rate-limit events, authentication events, payment anomalies and other risk indicators. These signals can be used for automated risk scoring and human review.

3. Social and creator content

Content submitted to VSBIL may be processed for storage, delivery, search/discovery, safety moderation, copyright or originality checks, quality assessment, fraud prevention and creator-program eligibility. Engagement metrics may be used to calculate distribution or revenue-sharing eligibility where the program provides for it.

4. Marketplace and shops

Shop owners may store storefront names, descriptions, logos, business contact information, products, SKUs, prices, stock and affiliate links. Customers may provide names, phone numbers, optional email addresses, delivery addresses, order items, payment status and tracking information. Public tracking is limited by design and requires an order number plus matching buyer phone.

5. WhatsApp Business

When a user connects their own WhatsApp Business number, VSBIL may process the phone-number ID, encrypted provider credential, bot configuration, keyword flows and message logs required to operate automation. Message logs can contain sender numbers, incoming messages, outgoing replies, timestamps and delivery/error information. Users connecting WhatsApp are responsible for appropriate notices, consent and third-party compliance.

6. Payments and financial records

VSBIL may process transaction references, amounts, currency, payment status, provider transaction identifiers and related reconciliation records. Payment credentials such as card details are handled by the payment provider rather than stored by VSBIL where provider-hosted checkout is used. Financial and audit records may be retained after account deletion when necessary.

7. How information is used

  • Authenticate accounts and maintain sessions.
  • Deliver feeds, campaigns, messaging, shops and business tools.
  • Verify activities, payments, referrals, rewards and withdrawals.
  • Operate order processing, delivery status and customer support.
  • Operate authorized WhatsApp automation.
  • Moderate content and investigate copyright, fraud and safety reports.
  • Protect the platform against automation, manipulation and account compromise.
  • Provide service notices, support and legally required communications.

8. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure and providers needed for a requested service, such as Supabase, Paystack, Google/YouTube, Meta/WhatsApp, Jumia, hosting, security, email and analytics providers. Information may also be disclosed where legally required, to protect users or investigate abuse, fraud or security incidents.

9. Automated decisions and human review

VSBIL may use automated rules and scores to flag suspicious activity, content, shops, payments or accounts. A flag can lead to additional verification, delayed rewards, restricted distribution, shop unpublishing, withdrawal review or other action. Where an appeal process is available, users can provide relevant evidence.

10. Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, security, financial reconciliation, customer disputes, fraud investigations, legal obligations and audit requirements. Different categories can have different retention periods.

11. Your controls and rights

Subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements, you may request access, correction, deletion or information about processing and may disconnect supported integrations. Privacy and security requests may require identity verification.

12. Public versus private information

Public profiles, public posts, published shops and published products can be discoverable according to their settings and the feature's design. Private account settings reduce discovery but cannot make information already lawfully shared with another user disappear from that user's records. Shop owners are responsible for lawful handling of customer information in their own commercial operations.

13. Cookies and browser storage

VSBIL may use essential cookies and browser storage for authentication, security, preferences and service operation. See the Cookie Policy.

14. International processing

VSBIL and its providers may process information in countries other than your location. Appropriate contractual, technical or organizational safeguards are used where required by applicable law.

15. Children

VSBIL is not intended to knowingly collect information from children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact support.

16. Changes and contact

This Policy may change as the product, providers, security practices or legal requirements change. For privacy requests, use Contact & Support or the secure support route.
