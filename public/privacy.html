Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Information we collect

We may collect account information such as email, username, referral information, activity submissions, wallet records, withdrawal details and security/audit information needed to operate the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, process activation payments, calculate rewards, review activities, prevent fraud, process withdrawals, provide support and improve security.

Payments

Payment card or mobile-money credentials are handled by the payment provider. VSBIL should not receive your card PIN or banking password.

Sharing

We may share necessary information with service providers that help operate authentication, hosting, payments, communications and security. We do not sell passwords.

Security

Protected server credentials must never be placed in browser code. Access to administrative functions is restricted by server-side authorization and audit logging.

Retention

Records may be retained as necessary for account operation, dispute handling, fraud prevention, financial reconciliation and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may contact support about account information, corrections or privacy requests. Some records may need to be retained for security or financial reasons.
