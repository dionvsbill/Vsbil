Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VSBIL collects, uses, stores, protects and discloses information when you create an account, use the VSBIL marketplace, connect Google or YouTube, make payments, earn rewards, request withdrawals, create campaigns, contact support or otherwise interact with the service.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to VSBIL websites, applications, APIs, dashboards, campaign tools, account services, communications and other products operated under the VSBIL service. It applies to users, campaign creators, advertisers, administrators and visitors to public pages.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

We may collect your name or display name, username, email address, password credentials in protected authentication systems, referral code, account status, role, verification state, profile information and account timestamps.

2.2 Verification information

We record verification events such as email verification, security verification and, where enabled, identity or payment-account verification. Verification codes are intended to be stored in a protected form and are subject to expiration and attempt limits.

2.3 Google and YouTube information

If you choose to connect Google or YouTube, VSBIL may receive the Google account identifier, authorized email address, YouTube channel identifier, channel title, channel URL, thumbnail and other information made available by the scopes you approve. OAuth access and refresh credentials, where required, are protected on the server and are not intended to be exposed to browser JavaScript.

VSBIL requests only permissions needed for the enabled integration. You can revoke access through Google and/or disconnect the integration through VSBIL. Revoking access may disable features that require YouTube authorization.

2.4 Activity and campaign information

We collect campaign records, task assignments, activity submissions, proof URLs where requested, verification results, reward amounts, review decisions and related timestamps. We use these records to operate the marketplace, prevent duplicate rewards and investigate disputes.

2.5 Financial information

We maintain records of activation payments, provider references, payment status, wallet movements, rewards, referral accounting and withdrawals. Payment providers may process payment credentials directly. VSBIL should not request your card PIN, banking password or payment-provider one-time password.

2.6 Security and technical information

Depending on the feature and configuration, we may process IP address, user-agent information, session identifiers, security events, authentication events, request identifiers, device/session metadata and timestamps. These records help detect unauthorized access, fraud, abuse and attacks against the platform.

2.7 Communications

We may retain support requests, notices, transactional emails, verification communications and responses needed to resolve account or payment issues.

3. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate and secure accounts.
  • Verify email addresses and security events.
  • Connect and maintain authorized Google/YouTube integrations.
  • Operate campaigns, tasks, submissions and eligibility rules.
  • Calculate rewards and maintain the wallet ledger.
  • Process and reconcile payments and withdrawals.
  • Operate referrals and prevent self-referral or duplicate rewards.
  • Detect fraud, automation, abuse, account compromise and payment anomalies.
  • Provide support, notices and service communications.
  • Maintain audit trails for administrative and financial actions.
  • Improve reliability, security and product quality.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting and dispute-resolution obligations where applicable.

4. Automated monitoring and risk decisions

VSBIL may use rules, scoring systems and automated checks to identify suspicious patterns such as repeated submissions, duplicate accounts, abnormal task completion, referral abuse, payment anomalies or unusual withdrawal behavior. These systems are intended to protect users and the platform, not to establish criminal guilt.

A security or risk flag may result in additional verification, delayed rewards, manual review, temporary withdrawal restrictions or account restrictions. Where an appeal process is available, users may provide information for human review.

5. YouTube and third-party services

VSBIL is independent of YouTube and Google and is not endorsed by them. When you authorize Google or YouTube, information is also governed by the relevant Google/YouTube terms and privacy practices. VSBIL does not guarantee that any activity will count toward YouTube monetization, eligibility or analytics goals.

Other service providers may include hosting, database, email, payment, security, analytics and advertising infrastructure providers. We limit information shared with providers to what is reasonably necessary for their service.

6. When we disclose information

We may disclose information to service providers acting on our instructions, payment providers for transaction processing, security providers, professional advisers, regulators or authorities where legally required, and parties involved in a corporate transaction where permitted. We do not sell passwords or intentionally expose private account credentials.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

VSBIL may use essential cookies or equivalent storage for authentication, security, preferences and service operation. Optional analytics or marketing technologies, if introduced, should be governed by the applicable consent and cookie controls. See the Cookie Policy.

8. Security

Security measures may include server-side authorization, encrypted transport, protected secrets, encrypted sensitive OAuth credentials, rate limiting, audit logging, database constraints, session controls, webhook verification and least-privilege access. No internet service can promise absolute security, so users should also protect passwords, devices and verification codes.

9. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including account operation, financial reconciliation, fraud prevention, dispute handling, security investigations and legal obligations. Financial ledgers and audit records may need to survive account deletion where retention is required or reasonably necessary.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements, you may request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion of an account, information about processing, or disconnection of Google/YouTube integrations. Some requests may require account verification before we act on them.

Deleting an account does not necessarily erase transaction, security, fraud-prevention or audit records that VSBIL must retain or has a legitimate reason to preserve.

11. Children

VSBIL is not designed to knowingly collect information from children who are below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child has created an account improperly, contact support so the matter can be reviewed.

12. International processing

VSBIL and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate contractual, technical or organizational safeguards should be used for international transfers.

13. Account deletion and data requests

Use the account settings or support channel provided by VSBIL to request deletion or privacy assistance. We may ask for reasonable verification to prevent unauthorized requests.

14. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the service, law or processing practices change. Material changes should be communicated through the service where appropriate. The version and effective date at the top identify the applicable edition.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or security concerns, use the Contact & Support page.
