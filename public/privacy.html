Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Effective August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VSBIL collects, uses, stores, protects and discloses information when you create an account, use campaigns, earn rewards, make payments, use Business Suite, operate a shop, connect WhatsApp Business, connect Google/YouTube, request withdrawals or contact support.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account information, verification events, campaign/activity records, payment and wallet records, security telemetry, support communications and information necessary to operate integrations. Security records can include IP address, user-agent information, request identifiers, session metadata and timestamps.

2. Google and YouTube

If you authorize Google or YouTube, VSBIL may receive only the information and scopes presented in the OAuth consent flow. Credentials are protected on the server and are not intended to be exposed to browser JavaScript. VSBIL is independent of Google and YouTube.

3. Business Suite information

Business users may store shop names, addresses, phone numbers, product catalogs, SKUs, inventory quantities, prices, invoices, tenant records, church offering records and funeral campaign records. VSBIL uses this information to provide the requested business tools, protect accounts, calculate platform fees where applicable and maintain service records.

4. Shop customer information

When a customer places a shop order, VSBIL may process the buyer name, phone number, optional email, delivery address, delivery notes, order items, payment status, order number and tracking information. Public order tracking is deliberately limited: a buyer must provide the order number and matching phone number. Shop owners receive customer information needed to fulfill the order and are independently responsible for lawful handling of that information.

5. WhatsApp Business credentials

When a user connects a WhatsApp Business number, VSBIL stores the supplied Meta access token in encrypted server-side storage. The raw token is not intended to be returned to the browser after connection. VSBIL may store the phone-number ID, display/business name, bot configuration, auto-reply flows and message logs needed to operate the automation.

WhatsApp message logs can contain phone numbers, incoming messages, matched keywords, outgoing replies, delivery status and error information. Users who connect WhatsApp are responsible for lawful notice and consent for their customers and compliance with Meta's policies.

6. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate and secure accounts.
  • Operate campaigns, activities, rewards and referrals.
  • Process payments, subscriptions, withdrawals and platform fees.
  • Operate business tools, storefronts, orders and tracking.
  • Run connected WhatsApp automation at the user's instruction.
  • Detect fraud, automation, abuse, account compromise and payment anomalies.
  • Provide support, notices and service communications.
  • Maintain audit trails and meet legal, accounting and dispute obligations.

7. Automated monitoring and risk decisions

VSBIL may use rules, scoring and automated checks to identify suspicious patterns. A risk flag may lead to verification, delayed rewards, manual review, withdrawal restrictions or account restrictions. These systems are intended to protect the platform and do not by themselves establish criminal guilt.

8. Third-party providers

Providers may include Supabase/database infrastructure, Paystack or other payment providers, Google/YouTube, Meta/WhatsApp, hosting, email, security and analytics services. We share information with providers only as reasonably necessary to deliver the relevant feature or satisfy legal obligations.

9. Security

Security controls may include HTTPS, server-side authorization, encrypted transport, encrypted sensitive credentials, rate limiting, audit logging, database constraints, session controls and webhook signature verification. No internet service can promise absolute security.

10. Retention

Information is retained as reasonably necessary for account operation, financial reconciliation, order fulfillment, fraud prevention, security investigations, dispute handling and legal obligations. Financial, security and audit records may need to survive account deletion.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements, you may request access, correction, deletion or information about processing, or disconnect supported integrations. Reasonable identity verification may be required. Shop owners are responsible for handling customer data requests relating to their own commercial records where the law places that responsibility on them.

12. Children and international processing

VSBIL is not designed to knowingly collect information from children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. VSBIL and service providers may process information in other countries where appropriate safeguards are used.

13. Cookies and storage

VSBIL may use essential cookies or browser storage for authentication, security, preferences and service operation. See the Cookie Policy.

14. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service, law or processing practices change. For privacy requests or security concerns, use Contact & Support.
