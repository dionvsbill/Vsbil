PRIVACY CONTROLS

Data Rights & Account Deletion

VSBIL aims to give users meaningful control over their information while preserving records required for security, financial reconciliation, disputes and legal obligations.

Access

You may request information associated with your account, subject to reasonable verification and applicable law.

Correction

Where supported, you can update profile information through account settings. You may contact support when information cannot be changed directly.

Google and YouTube access

You can disconnect supported Google/YouTube integrations from account settings and revoke authorization through Google. Disconnecting an integration may disable features that depend on it.

Deletion

You may request account deletion. VSBIL will process eligible deletion requests according to its operational and legal requirements. Information that must be retained for financial records, security investigations, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations may remain in protected systems for the applicable retention period.

Data export

Where an export feature is enabled, exported information may include account profile data, wallet transactions, activity history and other data reasonably associated with your account. Sensitive security information and third-party confidential information may be excluded where necessary.

Verification

We may verify account ownership before completing a privacy request. This protects users from someone attempting to obtain or delete another person's information.

Appeals and disputes

Deletion does not erase the historical facts required to investigate a payment dispute, fraud case or security incident. If a request affects an active dispute, support may explain the applicable retention requirement.

Contact

Use Contact & Support for privacy requests. See the complete Privacy Policy for additional information.
