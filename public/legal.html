Legal & Trust Center

Our policies explain how VSBIL operates accounts, campaigns, rewards, payments, withdrawals, connected services, safety systems and future advertising products.

Core policies

  • Privacy Policy — information collection, use, sharing, retention and privacy rights.
  • Terms of Service — the contractual rules governing use of VSBIL.
  • Community Guidelines — authentic, safe and respectful participation.
  • Acceptable Use — prohibited account, technical, financial and campaign behavior.
  • Withdrawal Policy — eligibility, review, limits and payout handling.
  • Refund Policy — payment correction and refund procedures.
  • Cookie Policy — cookies, sessions and optional technologies.
  • Security & Responsible Disclosure — platform security principles and reporting.
  • YouTube Participation Guidelines — genuine participation and third-party compliance.
  • Referral Policy — referral qualification and anti-abuse rules.
  • Advertising Policy — campaign advertising standards and future advertising access.
  • Data Rights & Account Deletion — access, correction, deletion and data requests.

Policy governance

Each policy has a version and effective date. Product changes, legal requirements, security improvements and third-party platform requirements may require revisions. Where a change materially affects users, VSBIL may provide an in-product notice or other appropriate communication.

Important platform distinction

VSBIL is an independent service. Google, YouTube, Paystack and other third-party providers are separate services with their own rules and decisions. VSBIL cannot guarantee third-party approval, availability, monetization eligibility or specific platform metrics.
