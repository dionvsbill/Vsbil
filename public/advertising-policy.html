ADVERTISING

Advertising Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

1. Advertising availability

VSBIL's advertiser portal is a controlled feature. The current product configuration is designed to remain disabled until the platform reaches 10,000 verified users and administrators enable the feature. Reaching the threshold does not itself guarantee access to Google Ads or another external advertising provider.

2. Advertiser responsibilities

Advertisers must provide truthful information, lawful products or services, accurate destinations and creatives they are authorized to use. Advertisers are responsible for complying with applicable advertising law and third-party advertising policies.

3. Prohibited advertising

  • Scams, phishing and deceptive financial schemes.
  • Malware, credential theft and malicious downloads.
  • Illegal products or services.
  • Impersonation and misleading claims.
  • Content prohibited by applicable law or the relevant advertising provider.
  • Ads designed to manipulate VSBIL rewards or third-party engagement.

4. Campaign review

Advertising submissions may be automatically screened and manually reviewed. VSBIL may request additional information, reject creatives, pause campaigns or restrict advertiser access when safety, legal or policy concerns exist.

5. Budget and payment

Where advertising is enabled, the advertiser should see the applicable price, budget and payment requirements before purchase. Payment confirmation must be verified server-side. Advertiser balances or budgets must never be controlled solely by browser code.

6. External advertising integrations

If VSBIL integrates with Google Ads or another provider, the integration will remain subject to that provider's approval, API permissions, account requirements, billing rules and advertising policies. VSBIL cannot override a provider decision.

7. Fraud and abuse

Advertisers must not generate artificial impressions, clicks, conversions or other fraudulent activity. VSBIL may suspend campaigns and accounts and preserve records necessary to investigate suspected abuse.

8. Refunds and campaign cancellation

Advertising refunds, unused budgets and campaign cancellations will be handled according to the applicable order terms and payment/refund rules presented when the advertising product is enabled.
