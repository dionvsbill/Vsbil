Cookie Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small browser values used to remember information between requests. VSBIL may also use equivalent browser storage or server-side session mechanisms where appropriate.

2. Essential storage

Essential storage may support authentication, session continuity, security controls, preferences and core application functionality. Disabling or clearing essential storage may sign you out or prevent some features from working correctly.

3. Security and fraud prevention

Session and security mechanisms may use short-lived identifiers, secure attributes and server-side records to help prevent unauthorized use. Security logs may separately record technical information as described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Preferences

Where implemented, preference storage can remember settings such as interface choices or dismissed notices. Preference data should not be used as a substitute for server-side authorization.

5. Analytics and marketing

If VSBIL introduces optional analytics, personalization or marketing technologies, the service should provide appropriate notice and controls before enabling technologies that require consent under applicable law. Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies.

6. Managing cookies

You can clear cookies and browser storage through your browser or device settings. You may also use available VSBIL privacy controls where provided. Removing essential cookies can invalidate sessions and require you to sign in again.

7. Changes

This policy may change when VSBIL introduces new technologies or changes how existing technologies are used.
