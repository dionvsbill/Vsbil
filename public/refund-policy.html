PAYMENTS

Refund & Payment Correction Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

1. Payment verification

Payment status is determined by server-side verification against the payment provider and VSBIL's transaction records. A browser redirect or client-side success message does not by itself create a successful payment record.

2. Activation payments

Where an activation payment has successfully activated an account, the payment is generally not refundable solely because the user later chooses not to use the service, except where a refund is required by applicable law or VSBIL approves an exception.

3. Duplicate or erroneous charges

If you believe you were charged more than once or charged without the expected service state, provide the payment reference. VSBIL will reconcile provider records and account records before determining the appropriate correction.

4. Failed payments

A failed or abandoned payment should not activate an account. If a provider temporarily reports an unclear state, VSBIL may wait for provider confirmation before making a final decision.

5. Unauthorized transactions

Report suspected unauthorized payments promptly. Do not send your card PIN, banking password or one-time authentication code to support staff. Payment-provider dispute procedures may also apply.

6. Refund method

Where a refund is approved, the method and timing may depend on the original payment provider and transaction. VSBIL may need to reconcile the original reference before initiating a correction.

7. Fraud and abuse

Refund requests must be truthful. Repeated fraudulent claims, chargeback abuse or attempts to obtain duplicate value may result in account restrictions and investigation.

8. Records

Payment and refund events may be retained for accounting, reconciliation, security, dispute handling and legal requirements.

9. Contact

Use Contact & Support for payment assistance.
