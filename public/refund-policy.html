VSBIL POLICY

Refund Policy

Activation payments are processed securely through our payment provider. A payment that has already activated an account is generally not refundable except where required by applicable law or where Vsbil determines a duplicate or erroneous charge occurred.

Payment issues

Contact support with your payment reference if you were charged but your account was not activated. We will verify the transaction server-side.

Unauthorized payments

Report an unauthorized transaction promptly so it can be investigated with the payment provider.
