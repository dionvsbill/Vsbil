ABOUT VSBIL

Built for genuine digital participation.

VSBIL is a platform for creators and participants to discover campaigns, complete eligible activities, earn platform rewards and build trusted digital participation records.

Our product principles

  • Authenticity: real people and transparent participation rather than bots or fabricated engagement.
  • Accountability: financial and administrative actions are recorded so important decisions can be investigated.
  • Security: payments, wallets, sessions, OAuth credentials and administrative actions are protected server-side.
  • Fairness: campaign allocation can use eligibility and rotation so opportunities are not permanently concentrated in a small group.
  • Clarity: users should understand rewards, limits, verification and policy requirements before acting.

How VSBIL works

Creators can publish eligible campaigns with defined actions, rewards and completion limits. Participants discover campaigns available to them, follow the stated instructions and submit proof where required. VSBIL's verification and risk systems determine whether a reward can be credited.

Wallet balances are backed by an internal transaction ledger. Withdrawals can be reserved while eligibility and risk checks are completed before payout.

Google and YouTube

VSBIL can provide optional Google and YouTube connectivity where enabled. These integrations use authorization flows rather than asking users for Google passwords. VSBIL is independent of Google and YouTube and does not guarantee monetization, subscriber, view or watch-time eligibility.

Advertising roadmap

VSBIL is designed to support an advertiser portal in the future. The current product configuration requires 10,000 verified users before the advertising feature is activated. Reaching the threshold does not automatically guarantee approval by an external advertising provider.

Responsible growth

We intend to grow the platform without compromising payment integrity, privacy or user safety. Features may therefore be introduced gradually, reviewed, rate-limited or temporarily disabled when operational or security conditions require it.
