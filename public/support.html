VSBIL Support

For account, activation payment, activity, withdrawal or privacy issues, provide the username/email connected to your account and a short description. Never send your password, card PIN, OTP or secret recovery codes.

Payment issue

Keep your Paystack reference and payment date. Support can use the reference to investigate the transaction.

Withdrawal issue

Include the withdrawal ID shown in your wallet history. Do not post full sensitive bank credentials in public channels.

Safety issue

Report impersonation, fraud, harassment or suspicious account activity promptly.
