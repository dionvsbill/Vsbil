REFERRAL PROGRAM

Referral Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

1. Purpose

The referral program rewards eligible users for introducing genuine new participants to VSBIL. It is not intended to reward account farms, self-referrals or artificial registrations.

2. Current launch rewards

Where the current launch configuration applies, a qualifying new user may receive 5 coins and the eligible referrer may receive 10 coins after the required qualification event. The platform configuration is authoritative and may change prospectively.

3. Qualification

A referral must be associated with a valid referral code and a genuinely new account. Qualification may require account verification and successful activation/payment according to the current program rules.

4. Prohibited referral behavior

  • Referring yourself or creating accounts for yourself.
  • Creating duplicate accounts to obtain multiple bonuses.
  • Buying, renting or trading accounts for referral purposes.
  • Using misleading advertising or impersonation.
  • Manipulating payment or activation events.
  • Coordinating fake registrations or referral rings.

5. Verification and reversal

Referral rewards may remain pending until qualification is confirmed. VSBIL may withhold or reverse rewards when a referral is found to be invalid, duplicated, fraudulent, reversed or inconsistent with the program rules. The wallet ledger records material reward movements.

6. Fair use

VSBIL may apply limits, risk controls and review procedures to protect the program and its participants. Referral availability is a platform feature and is not a guaranteed income source.

7. Disputes

Referral disputes should include the referral reference, relevant account information and a clear description of the issue. Do not disclose passwords or authentication codes to support staff.
