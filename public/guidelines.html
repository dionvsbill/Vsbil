PLATFORM STANDARDS

VSBIL Platform Guidelines

Version 1.0 · Effective August 29, 2026

Our standard

VSBIL is built around genuine participation, transparent rewards, responsible campaigns and respect for users and third-party platforms. These guidelines apply across account, campaign, wallet, referral and support features.

Account standards

Keep account information accurate. Protect credentials and verification codes. Use only accounts you control and never attempt to access another person's account.

Campaign standards

Campaigns must clearly describe the requested action, destination, reward and relevant conditions. Creators must not conceal material requirements or use deceptive links.

Participant standards

Complete tasks honestly and follow the instructions. Do not submit fabricated evidence, duplicate submissions or claims about actions that were not completed.

Financial standards

Do not manipulate activation payments, referrals, wallet balances or withdrawals. Never provide passwords, payment PINs or one-time authentication codes to another user or campaign creator.

Technical standards

Do not attack, overload, scan, exploit or bypass VSBIL systems. Automated access that manipulates rewards or defeats controls is prohibited.

Content standards

Do not use VSBIL to facilitate scams, impersonation, harassment, hate, exploitation, credential theft, malware, illegal transactions or other harmful activity.

Fair participation

Not every campaign is available to every user. Allocation can consider eligibility, campaign limits, rotation, safety and platform capacity. Attempting to circumvent those rules is prohibited.

Enforcement

VSBIL may remove content, reject submissions, reverse improper rewards, pause withdrawals, restrict features, suspend accounts or terminate accounts when evidence supports enforcement. Serious security or financial abuse may be escalated as appropriate.

Appeals

Where an appeal path exists, provide truthful and relevant evidence. Decisions may consider account history, ledger records, submission evidence, security events and campaign records.
