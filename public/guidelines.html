Community Guidelines

VSBIL is built around legitimate participation, transparent rewards and respect for platform rules.

Allowed

  • Use your own account and real activity.
  • Complete activities as described.
  • Submit truthful proof when requested.
  • Use only accounts and payment methods you are authorized to use.

Not allowed

  • Bots, automation, emulators or scripts designed to fake activity.
  • Multiple accounts used to manipulate rewards.
  • Self-referral or referral abuse.
  • Fake screenshots, altered proof or false claims.
  • Harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, scams or illegal content.
  • Attempts to access another user's account or the admin system.

Enforcement

We may reject submissions, reverse improper rewards, pause withdrawals, suspend accounts or ban accounts when there is evidence of abuse.
