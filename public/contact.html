DIRECT CONTACT

How can we
help you?

Send VSBIL a message directly. Your request is recorded securely and receives a reference you can keep for follow-up.

Never include passwords, OTPs, card PINs, private keys or full payment credentials.

BEFORE YOU SEND

Get a faster answer.

  • For payment issues, include the transaction reference.
  • For withdrawals, include the withdrawal reference and amount.
  • For campaigns, include the campaign reference or URL.
  • For account issues, use the email connected to your VSBIL account.
